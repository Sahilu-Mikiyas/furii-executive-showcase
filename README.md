# FURII Executive Showcase

FURII CTO PORTFOLIO — FRONTEND PRODUCT SPECIFICATION (v1.0)

1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Project Name

FURII — CTO Portfolio

Purpose

Create a premium digital experience that positions Mikiyas Sahilu as the CTO & Co-Founder of FURII, showcasing expertise in enterprise software architecture, AI integration, SaaS platforms, internal systems, and digital production.

This is not a portfolio. It should feel like the website of a company building world-class enterprise technology.

Visitors should leave believing they are looking at the public face of an executive leading a high-end technology company.

24. CONTENT STRATEGY

The site should tell a story:

Who is Mikiyas? → What is FURII? → What systems do we build? → How do we engineer them? → How does AI enhance them? → Why trust us? → Let's build together.

Every page should reinforce one central message:

FURII builds intelligent, beautifully engineered enterprise software for organisations that expect reliability, scalability, and exceptional user experience.

The portfolio should leave visitors with the impression that they are not browsing a freelancer's website, but the digital headquarters of a modern technology company led by a CTO who combines engineering discipline, product thinking, AI innovation, and design excellence into one cohesive vision.
